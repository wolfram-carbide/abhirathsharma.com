Minimalism isn't about removing everything—it's about removing everything that doesn't serve a purpose.

## The essence

Good minimalist design:
- Focuses on content
- Respects the user's attention
- Uses whitespace intentionally
- Chooses typography carefully

## Common mistakes

Bad minimalism:
- Removes useful features for aesthetics
- Prioritizes form over function
- Becomes sterile and lifeless
- Forgets about accessibility

## Finding balance

The goal is clarity, not emptiness. Every element should earn its place. If it doesn't serve the user or the content, it shouldn't be there.

This website is my attempt at that balance.
