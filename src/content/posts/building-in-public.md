There's something powerful about building in public. It's not just about showing the finished product—it's about documenting the journey.

## The benefits

When you build in public, you:
- Get feedback early and often
- Connect with like-minded people
- Create accountability for yourself
- Help others learn from your experience

## The challenges

It's not always easy. You have to be comfortable with:
- Showing work that's not perfect
- Receiving criticism
- Admitting when you're wrong
- Being vulnerable about what you don't know

But that's what makes it valuable.

## Moving forward

I'm committing to sharing more of my process—the messy middle, not just the polished end result.
