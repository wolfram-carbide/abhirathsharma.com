Visual essay · Inside a muscle fibre

Visualising strength and hypertrophy

How lattice stacking and size affect how much you lift. Inside a muscle fibre, thick filaments (myosin) have tiny heads that grab thin filaments (actin) and pull. The more heads that grip, and the more filaments there are, the more force the muscle makes.

Three training goals

Strength closest match

More of the heads grip. The muscle stays the same size.

How to train it

Heavy weights (about 85 to 95% of your one-rep max), 1 to 5 reps, long rests, maximum effort.

Hypertrophy closest match

More filaments, plus some fluid. The muscle gets bigger.

How to train it

Moderate weights taken close to failure, 5 to 30 reps, 10 or more hard sets per muscle each week.

Power closest match

Heads grip and let go faster. Same size, more speed.

How to train it

Light to moderate weights (about 30 to 60% of your one-rep max), or jumps and throws, moved as fast as possible. Few reps, full rests.

What changes inside the fibre

Examples for a 40 kg one-rep max. Real training mixes these changes, and fluid comes with new filaments, so strength still rises.
